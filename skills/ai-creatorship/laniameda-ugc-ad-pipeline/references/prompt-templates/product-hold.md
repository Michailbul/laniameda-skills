# Product Hold / Showcase Template

**Use when:** creator presents the product to camera — hero beauty shot, clean product-forward frame, no try-on or consumption. Used for brand ads where the product is the star and the creator is the trusted face next to it.

**Inputs required:**
- Image 1: persona reference
- Image 2: product image (clean, high-res, readable text)
- Optional image 3: composite reference of creator-holding-product (via Nano Banana Pro)

---

## Template

```
Multi-input video generation, Seedance V2.

SUBJECT: The woman from image 1, same face exactly, same features, do not alter
facial structure. She is holding the product from image 2 with both hands at
chest height, tilted slightly toward camera so the label reads cleanly.

PRODUCT: The exact product from image 2. All label text, logo, colors, and
proportions preserved. Product stays in sharp focus throughout. Do not warp or
alter the label.

ACTION: She holds the product steady for the opening beat, makes brief eye
contact with camera, then gestures toward the product with her right hand —
a small open-palm presentation move. Her expression opens from a soft closed-lip
neutral into a half-smile as she speaks. Eyes stay on camera 80% of the time,
flick down to the product briefly on the second sentence, then return.

DIALOGUE: She says
"[OPENING HOOK — casual, one short sentence]"
Brief pause, she glances at the product, then continues:
"[ONE SPECIFIC CLAIM — concrete, no hype]"
Ends with a small nod and a soft exhale.

CAMERA: Medium close-up, product and hands in frame, 50mm equivalent lens, shot
at eye level. Subtle push-in over the duration — start at chest-up framing,
end slightly tighter on face and product. Shallow depth of field, background
blurred.

LIGHTING: Clean soft natural light from camera-left, warm fill from camera-right,
no harsh shadows on the product label, label legible throughout.

ENVIRONMENT: [Neutral interior — clean kitchen counter / minimal bedroom / soft
studio backdrop]. Background is intentionally quiet so the product and face
dominate the frame.

AUDIO: Quiet room tone, clean vocal capture, no music, no ambient distractions.

CONSISTENCY: Face identical to image 1. Product identical to image 2 — text and
logo must remain sharp and unaltered. Hand positioning on product stable
throughout.

DURATION: ~6-8 seconds.
```

## Swap points

- Opening hook
- Specific claim
- Environment
- Camera movement (static / push-in / slow pan)

Keep constant:
- Persona (image 1)
- Product (image 2)
- Clean lighting rules
- Consistency lock

---

## Claim patterns that work

UGC feels real when claims are specific and oddly honest.

| Weak claim | Stronger claim |
|---|---|
| "This is amazing" | "Lasted me 3 weeks before I needed a refill" |
| "Best X ever" | "I've tried probably 8 of these — this is the one I actually finished" |
| "You'll love it" | "Not super sweet, which I wasn't expecting" |
| "Game-changing" | "Replaced three other things in my routine" |

Write the claim like you're texting a friend, not writing a press release.

---

## Failure watchouts

- **Label warp** — product text morphs during the clip. Fix: use highest-res product image available, add `"product label remains sharp and legible throughout, text does not warp"`.
- **Hands floating** — creator's grip on product looks unnatural. Fix: add `"natural grip, fingers visible around the product, product weight looks real"`.
- **Over-energetic delivery** — creator sounds like a QVC host. Fix: shorten sentences, add a pause cue, lower the expression intensity (closed-lip smile vs open smile).
- **Eye contact drift** — creator looks past the camera. Fix: add `"direct eye contact with camera for the majority of the clip, brief glance down to product only once"`.

---

## Variation axes

**Claim A/B:** same persona, same setup, swap the specific claim
**Creator A/B:** same product, same script, swap persona (use for demographic testing)
**Environment A/B:** clean kitchen vs minimal bedroom vs studio — test which vibe converts

## Example — Sirio's skincare-style product hold (inferred from the soda demo pattern)

What works in a product hold:
- Single clear hero shot, product in frame from second one
- Creator's expression supports the product, doesn't compete with it
- Dialogue is 2 short sentences max — not a speech
- Claim is specific ("not super sweet"), not general ("amazing")
- Cut ends on a soft beat, not a sales close

What breaks a product hold:
- Creator is too animated → reads as acting
- Product is cropped or tilted away from camera → why are we here
- Claim is vague → no reason to believe
- Creator smiles the whole time → uncanny
